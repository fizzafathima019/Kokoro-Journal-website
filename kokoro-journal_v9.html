App Features
Everything inside
the Kokoro app

The app is the smart layer — minimal screen time, maximum emotional clarity. Here's what it does.

Mood Check-In

Select your emotion, intensity, and trigger in 30 seconds. Simple visual inputs — no typing needed.

Activity Routing

Based on your mood, the app directs you to a specific page in your physical activity book instantly.

Mood Pattern Insights

Gentle weekly summaries showing when you feel best and what helps — presented simply, never overwhelming.

Book Access Unlock

Scan the QR code in your book to link it to the app. Each book purchase gives full, permanent app access.

Screen-Free by Design

The app's only job is to guide you to the book. Minimal interaction, maximum offline time.

Daily Check-In Streak

Optional gentle reminders to check in daily. Streaks are shown softly — no guilt, no pressure.

Safe Space — No Sharing

Your entries are completely private. No social feeds, no comparison, no judgment. Just you.

Student-First Design

Built for ages 15–25. Understands exam stress, emotional swings, and the need for something simple.
